Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about custody, coordinates verification, or security? Find fast solutions below.

How does QR coordinate verification work? +
CryoLedger matches the scanning device's live GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) with the product's pre-assigned geofence radius. Real-time verification ensures that details unlock only if checked at authorized hubs or retailers.
What happens during a product recall warning? +
If an item is actively recalled by the manufacturer/admin, the verification screen is immediately intercepted by a bright warning alert. Authorized locations are blocked from dispatching, and users receive nearest center return instructions.
What is the role of the Distributor vs Retailer in custody handover? +
The Distributor acknowledges shipment arrival at the logistics depot and authorizes dispatch to the assigned retailer center. The Retailer confirms receiving physical custody at the shop and performs the final handover/dispatch to the customer.
How can a user request a geofence location bypass? +
If the scan occurs in an unauthorized location, detail panels are hidden for safety. The verifier can click 'Request Administrative Bypass' coordinates authorization. An Admin can instantly review and approve from their dashboard panel.
